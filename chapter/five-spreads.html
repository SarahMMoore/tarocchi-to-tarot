How To Conduct a Spread

For contemporary web users visiting an educational mini-site, the massive multi-hour calculations of Book T are often streamlined into highly accessible, responsive layout configurations. Here is the definitive instruction set for performing a standard, foundational layout at home:

1. Formulating the Query

A successful reading depends entirely on clarity. The querent must focus on an open-ended, active question rather than a passive binary prompt:

  • Do not ask: "Will I get the job next Monday?"
  • Ask: "What energies should I focus on to improve my professional career trajectory?"

2. The Shuffling Sequence

  • Clear the workspace of clutter to enhance visual contrast against your card stock. Shuffle the 78-card deck using whatever physical shuffling style is preferred (overhand, riffle, or washing).
  • While shuffling, mentally hold the formulated query in focus to conceptually "program" the random array.
  • Place the deck face down and have the person receiving the reading cut the cards into three piles using their left hand, then restack them in any order.

Daily - The One-Card Pull

Best Used For: Daily mindfulness check-ins, rapid thematic insights, or isolated card-study meditation exercises.

Instructions

Deal a single card face up directly into the center of your table workspace.

Interpretation Method: This card does not represent a chronological storyline. Instead, read it as an overarching "environmental theme" or emotional filter for your current 24-hour cycle.

Past / Present / Future - The Three-Card Spread

Best Used For: Tracking a situation's structural timeline, discovering cause-and-effect patterns, and processing linear problem-solving queries.

Instructions

Deal three cards consecutively from left to right in a perfectly straight horizontal line:

  • Card 1 (Left Position) - The Past: Represents the root causes, previous choices, or historical forces that built the current situation.
  • Card 2 (Center Position) - The Present: Represents the immediate environment, active challenges, and the current state of awareness.
  • Card 3 (Right Position) - The Future: Projects the most likely logistical trajectory if choices and behaviors remain unaltered.

Interpretation Method: Do not read these as three separate, isolated meanings. Read them left-to-right like a sentence or storybook pages, observing how the visual themes flow from Card 1 into Card 3.

The Celtic Cross Spread - A Ten Card Layout

Best Used For: Comprehensive, deeply multi-layered analysis of a complex life situation. This layout utilizes 10 distinct cards to map a player's internal psyche against external environmental forces.

Instructions

The Central Cross Complex:
  • Card 1 (Center Base): Place this flat in the middle. Represents the immediate core situation or the querent's current baseline identity.
  • Card 2 (The Cross): Place this card horizontally directly over Card 1, forming a physical cross. This represents the immediate obstacle or active friction blocking progress.
The Surrounding Axis:
  • Card 3 (Below): Place directly beneath the central cross. Represents the deep subconscious roots or unseen foundation of the matter.
  • Card 4 (Left): Place to the immediate left of the central cross. Represents recent past events that are currently fading out of relevance.
  • Card 5 (Above): Place directly above the central cross. Represents conscious goals, idealized thoughts, or what the user is actively focusing on.
  • Card 6 (Right): Place to the immediate right of the central cross. Represents upcoming energies moving into the situation in the near future.
The Right-Hand Staff (The Vertical Column):

To the right of your central axis, lay down four final cards starting from the bottom and stacking upward:

  • Card 7 (Bottom of Staff) - The Self: Represents the reader’s inner emotional posture and true alignment.
  • Card 8 (Second up) - The External Environment: Represents social circles, workplace conditions, or the actions of other people.
  • Card 9 (Third up) - Hopes and Fears: Represents inner anxieties, psychological projections, or hidden aspirations.
  • Card 10 (Top of Staff) - The Ultimate Trajectory: Represents the final overarching resolution if all current components play out to completion.
