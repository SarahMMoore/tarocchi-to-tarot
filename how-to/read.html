Modern Spreads Instruction Set

For contemporary web users visiting an educational mini-site, these multi-hour calculations are streamlined into highly accessible, responsive layout configurations.

Foundational Mechanics Setup

  • Formulating the Query: A successful reading depends entirely on clarity. The querent must focus on an open-ended, active question rather than a passive binary prompt:
    • Do not ask: "Will I get the job next Monday?"
    • Ask instead: "What energies should I focus on to improve my professional career trajectory?"
  • The Shuffling Sequence: Clear the workspace of clutter to enhance visual contrast against your card stock. Shuffle the 78-card deck using your preferred physical style (overhand, riffle, or washing). While shuffling, mentally hold the formulated query in focus to conceptually "program" the random array. Place the deck face down and have the person receiving the reading cut the cards into three piles using their left hand, then restack them in any order.

The Three Most Common Layout Formats

The Daily One-Card Pull

  • Best Used For: Daily mindfulness check-ins, rapid thematic insights, or isolated card-study meditation exercises.
  • Layout Instructions: Shuffle and cut once with your left hand. Deal a single card face up directly into the center of your table workspace.
  • Interpretation Method: This card does not represent a chronological storyline. Instead, read it as an overarching "environmental theme" or emotional filter for your current 24-hour cycle.

Past, Present, Future Three-Card Pull

  • Best Used For: Tracking a situation's structural timeline, discovering cause-and-effect patterns, and processing linear problem-solving queries.
  • Layout Instructions: Deal three cards consecutively from left to right in a perfectly straight horizontal line.
  • Structural Positioning:
    • Card 1 (Left Position) — The Past: Represents the root causes, previous choices, or historical forces that built the current situation.
    • Card 2 (Center Position) — The Present: Represents the immediate environment, active challenges, and current state of awareness.
    • Card 3 (Right Position) — The Future: Projects the most likely logistical trajectory if choices and behaviors remain unaltered.
  • Interpretation Method: Do not read these as three separate, isolated meanings. Read them left-to-right like a sentence or storybook pages, observing how the visual themes flow from Card 1 into Card 3.

Celtic Cross Ten-Card Pull

  • Best Used For: Comprehensive, deeply multi-layered analysis of a complex life situation. This layout utilizes 10 distinct cards to map a player's internal psyche against external environmental forces.
  • Layout Instructions: Shuffle, cut, and distribute across two main visual arrays:
    • The Central Cross Complex:
      • Card 1 (Center Base): Place flat in the middle. Represents the immediate core situation or current baseline identity.
      • Card 2 (The Cross): Place horizontally directly over Card 1. Represents the immediate obstacle or active friction blocking progress.
    • The Surrounding Axis:
      • Card 3 (Below): Place directly beneath the central cross. Represents deep subconscious roots or unseen foundations.
      • Card 4 (Left): Place to the immediate left of the central cross. Recent past events currently fading out of relevance.
      • Card 5 (Above): Place directly above the central cross. Conscious goals, idealized thoughts, or active mental focus.
      • Card 6 (Right): Place to the immediate right of the central cross. Upcoming energies moving into the situation in the near future.
    • The Right-Hand Staff (The Vertical Column): To the right of the central axis, lay down four final cards starting from the bottom and stacking upward:
      • Card 7 (Bottom of Staff) — The Self: Represents the reader’s inner emotional posture and true alignment.
      • Card 8 (Second up) — The External Environment: Represents social circles, workplace conditions, or the actions of others.
      • Card 9 (Third up) — Hopes and Fears: Represents inner anxieties, psychological projections, or hidden aspirations.
      • Card 10 (Top of Staff) — The Ultimate Trajectory: Represents the final overarching resolution if all current components play out to completion.
