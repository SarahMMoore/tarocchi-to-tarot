The Major Arcana

The 1909 Rider-Waite-Smith Tarot Deck

  • 00 — The Fool

    • Attributes: New Beginnings | Spontaneity | Faith
    • Description: The Fool represents the true absolute genesis point of a major existential journey, representing raw innocence, boundless freedom, and stepping gracefully out into the terrifying unknown with an entirely open heart.

  • 01 — The Magician

    • Attributes: Manifestation | Willpower | Desire
    • Description: The Magician signals a position of absolute alignment where you hold full access to the elemental tools, conscious intellect, and divine capabilities required to turn physical goals into absolute concrete realities.

  • 02 — The High Priestess

    • Attributes: Intuition | Sacred Knowledge | Divine Feminine
    • Description: The High Priestess points towards deep internal landscapes, calling on you to pause your external actions, pull back from mundane distractions, and rely implicitly on your subconscious instincts and inner guidance layers.

  • 03 — The Empress

    • Attributes: Abundance | Nature | Nurturing
    • Description: The Empress embodies raw creation, domestic comfort, material abundance, and a profound connection to the comforting, cyclical cycles of the earth and nurturing unconditional maternal expressions.

  • 04 — The Emperor

    • Attributes: Authority | Structure | Regulation
    • Description: The Emperor represents systemic governance, structural stability, and the protective, paternal force of law. He stands as a monument to organizational order, establishing firm boundaries and solid foundations upon which civilizations are built.

  • 05 — The Hierophant

    • Attributes: Tradition | Orthodoxy | Education
    • Description: The Hierophant serves as the institutional bridge to historical legacy and sacred lore. He embodies conventional wisdom, spiritual mentorship, and the inherited philosophical codes passed down through scholarly lineages and formal systems of belief.

  • 06 — The Lovers

    • Attributes: Alignment | Duality | Discerning Choices
    • Description: The Lovers captures the profound tension of harmony, emotional reciprocity, and the convergence of dual forces. Beyond simple romantic ties, this framework points to critical decision thresholds that require a deeper alignment of core ethics and personal identity.

  • 07 — The Chariot

    • Attributes: Determination | Direction | Willpower
    • Description: The Chariot represents a state of focused acceleration and absolute control over opposing psychological forces. It demonstrates victory achieved not by chance, but through sheer internal resolve, alignment of purpose, and the active conquest of conflicting environmental obstacles.

  • 08 — Strength

    • Attributes: Fortitude | Endurance | Compassion
    • Description: Strength models power stripped of crude or violent force, favoring quiet endurance and soft mastery over primal instincts. It highlights the integration of our shadow desires through patience, mental stamina, and the graceful subduing of internal passions.

  • 09 — The Hermit

    • Attributes: Solitude | Introspection | Inner Guidance
    • Description: The Hermit illustrates a deliberate withdrawal from societal noise to map subjective psychological baselines. Carrying a singular lantern of truth, this archetype favors independent structural analysis, self-evaluation, and searching out ancient wisdom away from collective consensus.

  • 10 — Wheel of Fortune

    • Attributes: Cycles | Destiny | Karma
    • Description: The Wheel of Fortune underscores the mechanical reality of systemic loops, patterns, and inevitable structural decay. It serves as a reminder that external fortunes operate in volatile cycles, urging the observer to remain centered at the axis point rather than clinging to the spinning rim.

  • 11 — Justice

    • Attributes: Fairness | Truth | Law
    • Description: Justice represents the objective scales of truth, clarity, and cause and effect. It signals a period of rigorous self-examination, legal or ethical resolution, and the balancing of karmic debts where actions meet their exact, unvarnished consequences.

  • 12 — The Hanged Man

    • Attributes: Perspective | Surrender | Letting Go
    • Description: The Hanged Man represents a period of suspension, sacrifice, and seeing the world from a completely inverted angle. It signals a time to pause forward momentum, let go of control, and find spiritual enlightenment through surrender rather than struggle.

  • 13 — Death

    • Attributes: Transformation | Endings | New Beginnings
    • Description: Death represents the clearing away of the old to make absolute way for the new. It signals a profound phase of transition, urging you to release stagnant attachments, accept inevitable endings, and embrace the powerful rebirth that follows closure.

  • 14 — Temperance

    • Attributes: Balance | Moderation | Alchemy
    • Description: Temperance embodies the art of synthesis, blending opposing elements into a harmonious and integrated whole. It calls for patience, emotional stabilization, and the quiet alchemy of self-healing, guiding you to walk the middle path with calm purpose.

  • 15 — The Devil

    • Attributes: Bondage | Materialism | Shadow Self
    • Description: The Devil exposes the psychological chains of codependency, illusion, and unbridled materialistic attachment. It serves as a confrontation with your shadow self, revealing where you have willingly surrendered your personal autonomy to fear, habit, or instinctual obsession.

  • 16 — The Tower

    • Attributes: Sudden Change | Upheaval | Revelation
    • Description: The Tower represents the sudden, violent shattering of false constructs, prideful illusions, and unstable foundations. While terrifying, this sudden disruption clears away toxic frameworks instantly, offering an uncompromising baseline of absolute truth upon which to rebuild.

  • 17 — The Star

    • Attributes: Hope | Faith | Rejuvenation
    • Description: The Star pours forth a gentle stream of hope, cosmic inspiration, and deep spiritual healing after a period of immense turmoil. It acts as a serene guiding beacon, restoring your foundational faith in the universe and illuminating a peaceful path forward.

  • 18 — The Moon

    • Attributes: Illusion | Anxiety | Subconscious
    • Description: The Moon navigates the hazy, nocturnal realm of anxiety, psychological projections, and hidden truths. It warns against taking things at face value, urging you to walk through the wild landscape of uncertainty by relying heavily on dreams and emotional intelligence.

  • 19 — The Sun

    • Attributes: Vitality | Success | Radiance
    • Description: The Sun represents a state of unmitigated clarity, joy, and conscious realization. It radiates pure cosmic vitality, warmth, and public celebration, validating your personal truth and illuminating every dark corner with absolute transparency and success.

  • 20 — Judgement

    • Attributes: Absolution | Calling | Awakening
    • Description: Judgement signals a profound spiritual awakening and a definitive moment of reckoning. It is a cosmic summons to rise above past mistakes, integrate your life's collective lessons, and step forward into a higher, self-actualized purpose with a clean slate.

  • 21 — The World

    • Attributes: Completion | Integration | Wholeness
    • Description: The World represents the ultimate culmination of the entire existential journey, marking a state of absolute wholeness, integration, and cosmic closure. It celebrates the successful mastery of a major cycle, bringing a deep sense of belonging and fulfillment.
