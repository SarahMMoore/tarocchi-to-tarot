Archival Sources & Literature Reference Registry

The following academic registry lists the historical manuscripts, guild manifests, and literary compositions utilized to build the historical curriculum across this platform:

  • Chapters 1 & 2: Origins and Anatomy of Playing Cards
    • International Playing-Card Society. (2018). Historical Development of Playing Cards: Technical Archives. IPCS Monographs.
    • Needham, J. (1985). Science and Civilisation in China: Volume 5, Chemistry and Chemical Technology; Part 1, Paper and Printing. Cambridge University Press. (Documenting late Tang Dynasty royal court chronicles on yezi xi).
    • Mayer, L. A. (1971). Mamluk Playing Cards. E. J. Brill. (Detailing 13th-century Egyptian calligraphic suit designs and transcontinental migration metrics).
    • Dummett, M. (1980). The Game of Tarot: From Ferrara to Salt Lake City. Duckworth. (Analyzing 15th-century Italian guild records of cartegiari and municipal gaming edicts).
  • Chapters 3 & 4: Rules of Tarocchi and Mystical Transitions
    • Petrarca, F. (c. 1374). I Trionfi [The Triumphs]. Northern Italian Allegorical Poem Archive. (Mapping structural card alignments from Love to Eternity).
    • Court de Gébelin, A. (1781). Le Monde Primitif, Analysé et Comparé avec le Monde Moderne (Vol. 8). Paris. (Sourcing the original essay establishing the Egyptian Book of Thoth hypothesis).
    • Alliette, J.-B. [Etteilla]. (1789). Les sept nuances de l'œuvre philosophique du jeu des tarots. Paris. (Sourcing reversed semantic keywords and modern cartomancy spread layouts).
    • Lévi, É. (1854). Dogme et Rituel de the Haute Magie. Hippolyte Baillière. (Documenting the direct linguistic mapping of Major Arcana keys onto the 22 letters of the Hebrew Alphabet).
    • Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn. (1888). Manuscript Book T: The Tarot. London. (Sourcing multi-tiered layouts, elemental dignities, and the Opening of the Key ritual framework).
  • Chapters 5 & 6: Modern Spreads and Data Sciences
    • Waite, A. E. (1911). The Pictorial Key to the Tarot. William Rider & Son, Ltd. (Detailing the artistic architectures and 10-card Celtic Cross blueprint).
    • Jung, C. G. (1959). The Archetypes and the Collective Unconscious (Collected Works Vol. 9, Part 1). Princeton University Press. (Validating card layouts as projected mirrors of human psychological themes).
    • Leibniz, G. W. (1703). Explication de l'Arithmétique Binaire. Royal Academy of Sciences. (Establishing historical connections between binary architectures, structural data modeling, and ancient symbolic systems).
